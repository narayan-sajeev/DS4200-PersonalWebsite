About Me

I am a junior undergraduate student at Northeastern University studying Data Science and Business Administration with a Concentration in Finance. I am originally from New Hampshire, a short drive from Boston. A lot of my coursework involves working with numbers and writing code to visualize datasets and make forecasts. I have taken several classes throughout my time in college to learn how to use Python and SQL to analyze data. I enjoy studying how data analysis can be applied to solve business problems and help make financial decisions. As technology becomes increasingly incorporated into finance, I am excited to see what the future will look like.

Outside of analysis, I hope to learn more about how insights can be effectively communicated so that people can make the right decisions. Large datasets are usually difficult to interpret, so learning how to use visualizations is important. In my classes, I'm learning about how to use HTML and CSS to create websites that display charts online. In the future, I hope to work in roles where I can apply my quantitative background to finance problems. When I'm not in class or studying, I enjoy spending time working out at the gym, taking photos around the city, and hanging out with friends. I also love watching movies and trying out new recipes. I built this website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for my homework to show proficiency with front-end web design.
