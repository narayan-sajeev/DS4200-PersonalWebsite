My Hobbies and Weekly Routine

I keep a balanced schedule throughout the semester to avoid burnout. Classes and homework can take up a lot of my day, so I stick to a set routine to stay organized. I try to take time out of my schedule every day to go to the gym and work out, because having that break from staring at a laptop gives me a lot more energy. Even if I am extremely busy, I'll still make sure to leave my room and go on a walk to decompress and get a breath of fresh air. Another thing that I found really helps me deal with intense work is spending time with others. Whenever I get stressed by looming assignments and tests, I spend a few minutes talking to my friends, and that always helps me feel better. I also found that watching movies helps me relax.

Besides exercise, I also love taking photos of Boston as I walk around. Using a dedicated camera allows you to take more refined photos compared to a standard phone camera. I've been taking photos for several years now, and I am still learning every single day. I also enjoy playing poker with friends, which is a fun way to apply probability and statistics in a real-world situation.
