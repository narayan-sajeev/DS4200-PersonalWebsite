My Hobbies and Weekly Routine

It's important to keep a balanced schedule throughout the semester to ensure you're not getting burnt out. Classes and homework can take a lot of time out of your day, so sticking to a set routine helps me stay organized. I try to take time out of my schedule every day to go to the gym and work out, because having that break from staring at a laptop gives me a lot more energy.

Besides exercise, I also love taking photos of Boston as I walk around. Using a dedicated camera allows you to take more refined photos compared to a standard phone camera. I also enjoy playing poker with friends, which is a fun way to apply probability and statistics in a real-world situation.
